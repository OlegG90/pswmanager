PswManager · Android · UI audit · 3 November 2026

Mockups, code and spec

Compares the v1 mockups (PswManager Android.dc.html), the app (apps/android/ui/src) and docs/spec-android.md at version 0.20.0, and records what was decided. Where a mockup and the spec disagree, the spec wins.

1 · Structure: where v1 differed, and what v2 shows

The v1 mockups came before the code and fell behind it. v2 follows the code on all of these.

2 · The look: Classical against style.css

The desktop moved to WinUI 3, so the Android app is the only one on Classical, and its stylesheet is its own: changing it does not touch Windows. The sizes and contrast below are worked out from style.css.

WhatCode at 0.20.0Decision
Tap feedbackNo :active or :focus-visible, and the system's highlight is off: a tap shows nothingFix: pressed and focus states
Tap targetsDrawer rows about 32px, toolbar icons 36px tall, line icons 40pxFix: 44–48px
Accent text#b68235 on the ground is 3.02:1 in the light theme (the dark theme's accent is 7.49:1)Fix: text takes accent-700 (#7d5411, 5.97:1)
--color-neutral-500Used by the drawer's version and a tag's #, never declared, so neither is mutedFix: use neutral-700; Classical's 500 is about 2.6:1, too faint for text
Primary button, floating buttonsFilled with the accent; floating buttons with a 16px radius and a heavy shadowTake Classical's outline; floating buttons stay 56px on the opaque ground, with --shadow-md
Radii, tags, weight4, 6, 8, 16 and 999px; pill tags; bold headingsTake 4px and accent-800 on tags, and 600 at most. The bottom sheet keeps 12px
Colour tokens8 of them; the light surface and accent-100 differ from Classical'sAdd only those in use, not the whole ramps. Fields stay on white, which reads better
FontsSystem fontsStay: a decision of the project, and v2 follows it
Selects, icons, justified textNative selects, the app's own strokes, flush-left textStay: the cost or the fit on a phone outweighs what they would bring
CheckboxesNative, 22pxSwitches as in v2 can come later, in CSS only
The master password fieldNo eyeAdd one: a long password is easy to mistype on a phone

3 · The mockups themselves

The data in v2 is made up. The unlock screen's sync line and the rows of the Autofill, Sync and About tabs were corrected against main.ts; v1 and the desktop prototype keep their older sample data.

On the spec: the status line was described both at the bottom and on top (it is at the bottom), and its mockup numbers pointed at v1. Both are corrected in this branch.
